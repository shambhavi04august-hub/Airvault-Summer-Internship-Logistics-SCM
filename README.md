# 🔎 Problem Statement

Growing customer expectations and global trade make **transportation, documentation, shipment tracking, and interdepartmental coordination** increasingly important to logistics performance.

The study examined how Airvault manages these activities and where **operational efficiency and service quality** could be strengthened.

During the internship, I worked on:

* Managing shipping documentation

* Sharing shipment status updates
* Assisting air freight coordination
* Assisting sea freight coordination
* Supporting customs documentation and compliance checks
* Coordinating with operations, customer service, warehouse, and transport teams

# 📊 Key Findings

* Airvault follows a structured logistics and supply chain process.
* Interdepartmental coordination supports smooth shipment execution.
* Digital tracking improves shipment visibility and customer satisfaction.
* Accurate documentation and customs compliance help reduce delays.
* Timely and customer-centric service strengthens market position.

---

# 💡 What the Internship Revealed

The internship demonstrated that operational performance depends on coordination across:

**Freight + Documentation + Customs + Customer Service**

Key learnings included:

* Technology improves shipment visibility and decision-making.
* Documentation accuracy is a critical control point.
* Customer communication is closely linked with perceived service quality.
* Data-driven monitoring can support continuous process improvement.
